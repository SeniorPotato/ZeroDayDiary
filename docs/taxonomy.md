# ZeroDayDiary Taxonomy

## Categories

### Security
Cyber incidents, exploit activity, software compromise, ransomware, supply-chain failures, credential theft, and operational defensive changes.

### Privacy
Data misuse, surveillance expansion, data broker exposure, consent failures, cross-border transfer issues, privacy enforcement, and tracking changes.

### AI Risk
Model misuse, safety incidents, autonomy concerns, governance failures, model deployment harms, red-teaming gaps, and operational misuse patterns.

### Governance
Policy action, standards, regulator intervention, enforcement, court decisions, public consultations, and institutional accountability.

### Infrastructure
Cloud, telecoms, energy, transport, government systems, healthcare infrastructure, and other systems where failure has broad impact.

### Surveillance
Biometric systems, facial recognition, device tracking, location monitoring, automated enforcement, and state or commercial observation systems.

## Tagging rules
- Use **1 primary category tag** from the category set.
- Add **2-4 supporting tags** only.
- Prefer specificity over volume.
- Use stable lowercase kebab-case style.
