# ZeroDayDiary Source Map

## Primary-source tiers

### Tier 1 — official / primary
Use first whenever possible.
- national cybersecurity agencies (e.g. CISA, NCSC)
- data protection regulators
- court filings and government notices
- official company incident disclosures
- public procurement and standards documents
- legislative or regulator consultations

### Tier 2 — trusted specialist reporting
Use for discovery and context, then verify.
- established cybersecurity trade publications
- privacy-focused investigative outlets
- policy and governance reporting
- major international news wires

### Tier 3 — broad discovery only
Use only as prompts for verification, not as final authority.
- aggregators
- vendor commentary
- general tech news with thin sourcing
- social posts without linked evidence

## Editorial workflow
1. discover a candidate event
2. locate primary or near-primary source
3. classify using taxonomy
4. draft event entry
5. publish through reviewable Git workflow

## Priority source buckets
### Security
- breach disclosures
- CERT advisories
- ransomware and incident notifications
- exploit and patch advisories

### Privacy
- regulator announcements
- surveillance policy changes
- data-sharing or tracking changes
- enforcement notices

### AI Risk
- deployment incidents
- safety control failures
- policy interventions
- misuse cases with public evidence

### Governance / Infrastructure
- public-sector cyber programs
- critical infrastructure incidents
- standards, court, and enforcement action
