## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted nikkei discloses breaches of employees’ microsoft, google email accounts. Over the weekend, Japanese publishing giant Nikkei disclosed that unknown attackers recently breached two employee email accounts and used one to send thousands of phishing emails. In a Sunday statement, the company said an employee's Google Workspace account was accessed in late July, exposing the personal information of employees and business partners.
