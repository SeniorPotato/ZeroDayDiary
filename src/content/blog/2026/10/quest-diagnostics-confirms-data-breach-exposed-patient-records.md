## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted quest diagnostics confirms data breach exposed patient records. American clinical laboratory company Quest Diagnostics disclosed that patient records have been exposed after a threat actor gained unauthorized access to its Quanum LSM system. The company learned about the breach on October 21 and deployed its incident response protocol "to contain the activity, assess and investigate the nature and scope of the incident and implement appropriate remedial measures.".
