## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted widespread php flaw may cause many site outages today. Expect to see quite a few websites serving error pages today: Nearly 70 percent of websites need to update their core software to fix a pair of vulnerabilities that allow an attacker to seize full control over affected servers. The flaws at issue are present in PHP, the programming language that powers sites running on a large number of web publishing systems, including Drupal, Magento, Joomla and WordPress.

## Why it matters
This matters because AI-related risk increasingly shows up through deployment choices, interfaces, and governance gaps rather than model headlines alone.
