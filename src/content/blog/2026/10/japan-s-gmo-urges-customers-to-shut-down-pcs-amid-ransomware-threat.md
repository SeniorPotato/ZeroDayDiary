## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted japan's gmo urges customers to shut down pcs amid ransomware threat. The GMO Group has urged customers to disconnect their systems from the internet and shut down computers after a threat actor who breached the company’s Onamae Desktop Cloud threatened to launch a ransomware attack today. The company later confirmed that data belonging to both the company and its customers had been published online.
