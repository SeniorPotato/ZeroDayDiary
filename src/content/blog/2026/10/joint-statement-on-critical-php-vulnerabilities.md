## What happened
The latest enisa publication sets out a development that is directly relevant to security operators. The European Commission (DG CNECT), ENISA, the European Union Agency for Cybersecurity, CERT-EU, Europol EC3, and the network of the EU national computer security incident response teams (CSIRTs network), advise organisations to take immediate action to address two critical vulnerabilities in PHP, a programming language widely used for websites and applications. The vulnerabilities, tracked as CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, could allow attackers to remotely execute malicious code on vulnerable servers without authentication.
