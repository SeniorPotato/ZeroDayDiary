## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted hackers use autonomous ai tool cairn to find zero-day, take over server. Hackers used an autonomous AI agent platform called "Cairn" to discover a zero-day vulnerability in a medical imaging application and exploit it to take complete control of a server. the suspected Chinese-linked attackers simply instructed the AI agents to "obtain shell access," leaving them to independently discover and exploit the flaw.

## Why it matters
This matters because AI-related risk increasingly shows up through deployment choices, interfaces, and governance gaps rather than model headlines alone. It is a direct signal about how compliance and policy expectations are being translated into implementation work.
