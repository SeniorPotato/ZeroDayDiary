## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted synology warns of c3rb3r ransomware attacks targeting nas devices. Taiwanese network-attached storage (NAS) appliance maker Synology warned customers to update their devices immediately due to a surge in C3RB3r ransomware attacks. The company urged users to enable automatic updates and upgrade the DiskStation Manager (DSM) software running on their NAS devices to the latest version to protect their data.
