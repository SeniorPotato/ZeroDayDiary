## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted data breach impacts 40 irish credit unions in reported it provider hack. A reported cyberattack on Wellington IT has exposed sensitive information belonging to members of 50 credit unions, the majority of which are located in Ireland. Wellington IT is a Belfast-based supplier whose services are used by about 50 credit unions, including 43 in Ireland.
