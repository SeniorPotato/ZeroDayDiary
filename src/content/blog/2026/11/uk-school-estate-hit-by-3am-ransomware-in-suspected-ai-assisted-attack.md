## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted uk school estate hit by 3am ransomware in suspected ai-assisted attack. A 3AM ransomware affiliate targeted a school estate in the UK and successfully encrypted around 37 of nearly 50 machines reached from an unmanaged host. Researchers at cybersecurity company Bitdefender believe the threat actor may have used AI-generated commands and an automated agentic loop to carry out the operation.
