## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted ukraine, fbi arrest ransomware gang members tied to $40m in ransoms. Ukrainian authorities, working with the FBI, have arrested four suspected members of an international ransomware organization linked to more than $40.5 million in ransom payments from at least 28 companies worldwide. The arrests come after a two-year investigation into a cybercrime organization comprising at least 27 individuals from Ukraine, Kazakhstan, Russia, and other countries.
