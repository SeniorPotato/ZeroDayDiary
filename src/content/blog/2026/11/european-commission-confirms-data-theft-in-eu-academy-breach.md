## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted european commission confirms data theft in eu academy breach. The European Commission, the European Union's main executive body, has confirmed that attackers breached its EU Academy online learning hub and stole the personal data of an undisclosed number of users. This follows the Commission's Thursday disclosure that hackers breached its systems on October 22 using the ARTEX AI-powered penetration testing framework, causing service disruptions that also affected its internal Sysper HR platform.
