## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted who runs the ransomware group qilin?. The cybercrime group Qilin has emerged as the world’s most active ransomware affiliate program, claiming at least 365 victim organizations in the past three months alone. Operating since September 2022, the Russia-based ransomware group Qilin is the currently the most industrious ransomware-as-a-service gang, according to a report (PDF) this month by the cybersecurity firm NCC Group (KrebsOnSecurity profiled the administrator of the second most active ransomware group — The Gentlemen — in June).
