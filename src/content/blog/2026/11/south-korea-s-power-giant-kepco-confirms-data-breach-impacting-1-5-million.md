## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted south korea’s power giant kepco confirms data breach impacting 1.5 million. A threat actor breached the internal network of Korea Electric Power Corporation (KEPCO) and accessed two systems containing customer, employee, and contractor data. The attack exposed details of more than 1.5 million individuals before the company blocked access from external IP addresses and suspended the affected services.
