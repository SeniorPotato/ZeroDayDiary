## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted cto of ‘ransomwarehelp’ indicted for wire fraud. The chief technology officer and co-founder of ransomwarehelp.com, a six year old Florida business that claimed to use “advanced cryptographic tools” to restore data locked by ransomware, has been charged with wire fraud after an FBI sting operation found the company was simply paying the attackers for a decryption key and charging victims a markup for work that was never performed. The website RansomwareHelp urged victims “Do not pay the ransom!,” claiming its advanced cryptographic tools could restore 100 percent of data, servers, and systems to their original format, and that there was no need to give in to extortion demands.
