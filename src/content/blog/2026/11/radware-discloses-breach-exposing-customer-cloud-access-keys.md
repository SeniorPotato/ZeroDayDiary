## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted radware discloses breach exposing customer cloud access keys. Cybersecurity company Radware says attackers gained access to some of its customers' account details and cloud access keys through an unsecured internal Cloud WAF (Web Application Firewall) management API that was left exposed online. Founded in 1996 and headquartered in Tel Aviv, Radware now provides application delivery and DDoS protection services to more than 12,500 enterprise and telecom carrier customers worldwide.
