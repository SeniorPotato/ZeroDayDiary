## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted five warning signs of ransomware breach before encryption or data theft. Ask many IT teams how they would recognize ransomware, and the answer is still “when files start changing extensions.” By then, the intrusion may already include credential theft, privilege escalation, defense evasion, recovery sabotage, lateral movement and data exfiltration. Encryption is often a late-stage action, not the beginning of the attack.

## Why it matters
This matters because it changes what privacy teams, platform owners, or product leaders should treat as a real operating constraint.

## Assessment
The strongest signal here is that a vulnerability class or attack path is being treated as operationally relevant rather than background technical debt. In practice, that means cloud-adjacent control planes, shared services, and inherited trust assumptions deserve more scrutiny than many organisations currently give them.

## Recommended actions
- Review whether the issue, advisory, or attack pattern is relevant to your environment, suppliers, or exposed systems
- Patch, harden, or validate logging and monitoring coverage where applicable
- Check whether cloud services, connectors, or shared administrative paths create avoidable trust-boundary risk
- Monitor follow-on reporting or primary-source updates for scope expansion, implementation guidance, or stronger enforcement signals
