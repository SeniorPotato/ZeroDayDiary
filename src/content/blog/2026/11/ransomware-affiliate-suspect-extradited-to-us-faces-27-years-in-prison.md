## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted ransomware affiliate suspect extradited to us, faces 27 years in prison. A Kazakh national has been extradited from Italy to face charges alleging he targeted US schools, hospitals, and healthcare entities in ransomware attacks. 43-year-old Roman Khlynovskiy was an alleged member of the "8-Digits Team" ransomware group, known for its affiliation with the INC and Lynx ransomware operations.
