## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted d-link warns of multiple zero-days in dgs-3130 series switches. D-Link shared mitigation measures for multiple unpatched security flaws affecting DGS-3130 series managed Gigabit stackable switches. These switch models provide multi-gigabit uplinks, allowing enterprise network administrators to eliminate data bottlenecks, and are designed primarily for small-to-medium businesses (SMBs) and metro Ethernet deployments.
