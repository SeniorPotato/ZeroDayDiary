## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted hackers hijack cpguard autoupdate to deploy backdoor, steal data. Attackers have compromised the update infrastructure for the cPGuard web security suite to distribute a backdoor and a data-stealing script. OpsShield, the cPGuard developer, published an alert earlier today saying the attack began on November 2 at 02:06 UTC and affected versions 5.88.12 through 5.88.19.
