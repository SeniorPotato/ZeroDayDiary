## What happened
The latest analysis post sets out a development that is directly relevant to security operators. The video describes a technique that can reveal where an otherwise unidentifiable recording was made by analyzing its audio for the faint sounds of passing aircraft. It works by looking at Doppler curves, which plot the way a plane’s sound changes as it passes overhead because of the Doppler effect.
