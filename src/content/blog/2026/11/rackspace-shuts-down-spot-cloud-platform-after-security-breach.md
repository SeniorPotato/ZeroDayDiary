## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted rackspace shuts down spot cloud platform after security breach. Rackspace is shutting down its Rackspace Spot cloud platform after a security breach allowed hackers to access customer Kubernetes clusters and secrets, giving customers until December 8 to migrate their services and download their data. Rackspace Spot is a cloud platform that allows organizations to deploy and manage containerized applications using Kubernetes.
