## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted mount royal university confirms breach as hackers claim attack. Mount Royal University in Calgary says hackers stole and then deleted data from its file storage systems after breaching the university's network. In an update published on its website, MRU states that it has engaged technical teams and external cybersecurity experts to investigate the incident and to support recovery efforts following a cyberattack on June 17.
