## What happened
The latest analysis post sets out a development that is directly relevant to security operators. Outsider Enterprise operates through Telegram. Google worked with AT&T, Verizon, and T-Mobile to block many of these malicious text messages, and Google notes that its on-device scam detection in Google Messages probably helped reduce the number of successful phishing attempts, too.
