## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted estée lauder discloses data breach via oracle e-business flaw. Cosmetics giant Estée Lauder is notifying customers of a data breach after hackers exploited a flaw in Oracle E-Business Suite that the company used for human resources (HR) operations. The company says that last month it identified an intrusion that had occurred on August 9, 2025, which led to the threat actor obtaining " personal information of certain individuals.".
