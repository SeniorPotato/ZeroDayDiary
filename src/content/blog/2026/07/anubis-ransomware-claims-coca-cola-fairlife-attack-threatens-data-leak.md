## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted anubis ransomware claims coca-cola fairlife attack, threatens data leak. The Anubis ransomware gang has claimed responsibility for the cyberattack on Coca-Cola's Fairlife dairy subsidiary, threatening to publish allegedly stolen corporate data unless the company pays a ransom. Fairlife is one of Coca-Cola's dairy brands and produces a range of ultra-filtered milk products, protein shakes, and nutrition drinks sold throughout the United States.
