## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted former ransomware negotiator gets 4 years for blackcat attacks. A former employee of cybersecurity incident response company DigitalMint was sentenced to 70 months in prison for targeting U.S. The FBI linked the BlackCat ransomware gang to more than 60 breaches between November 2021 and March 2022, adding in a separate advisory that the cybercrime group had collected at least $300 million in ransom payments from more than 1,000 victims through September 2023.
