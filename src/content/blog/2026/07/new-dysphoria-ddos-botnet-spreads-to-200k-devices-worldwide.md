## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted new dysphoria ddos botnet spreads to 200k devices worldwide. A botnet called Dysphoria has compromised around 200,000 devices across the world and is using them for distributed denial of service (DDoS) attacks and traffic relay operations. Dysphoria evolved from the ‘jackskid’ and ‘fbot' malware by adding a covert blockchain-based command-and-control (C2) resolution mechanism.
