## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted dhs confirms hackers breached hsin info-sharing platform. The Department of Homeland Security is investigating a cyberattack that compromised the Homeland Security Information Network (HSIN), a sensitive information-sharing platform used by federal, state, local, and private-sector partners. The intrusion, first reported by Nextgov, was carried out by an unknown threat actor in recent weeks and is believed to have occurred sometime between late May and early June, according to two people familiar with the matter who spoke on the condition of anonymity.
