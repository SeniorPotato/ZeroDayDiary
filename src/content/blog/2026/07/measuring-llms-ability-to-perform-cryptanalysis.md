## What happened
The latest analysis post sets out a development that is directly relevant to security operators. There’s new benchmark measuring AI’s ability to perform mathematical cryptanalysis. The benchmark: “CryptanalysisBench: Can LLMs do Cryptanalysis?” The idea is to benchmark the ability of LLMs to discover new mathematical cryptanalytic attacks against a series of historical algorithms.
