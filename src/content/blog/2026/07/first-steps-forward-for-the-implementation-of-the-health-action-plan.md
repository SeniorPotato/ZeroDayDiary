## What happened
The latest enisa publication sets out a development that is directly relevant to security operators. ENISA signed a Contribution Agreement with the European Commission for the Agency to support the health sector in building robust cybersecurity defenses against cyber threats. Launched in 2025 by the European Commission, the EU Action Plan for the cybersecurity of hospitals and healthcare providers was a pivotal step towards enhancing the protection and resilience of the sector.
