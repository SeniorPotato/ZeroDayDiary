## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted whatsapp phishing attack uses fake business docs to hack pcs. An ongoing malware campaign is targeting WhatsApp users in multiple countries with deceptive messages that push VBScript files, leading to remote system access. The threat actor is using file names that indicate business and financial documents delivered by the victim's contacts, whose accounts had been compromised.
