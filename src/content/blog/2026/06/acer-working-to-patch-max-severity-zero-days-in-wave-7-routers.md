## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted acer working to patch max severity zero-days in wave 7 routers. Acer confirmed that it's working to address two maximum-severity zero-day vulnerabilities affecting its Wave 7 mesh routers. the two security flaws were reported by security researcher Gergo Pap and affect Wave 7 routers running firmware version T7c_GBL_1.01.000055 or earlier.
