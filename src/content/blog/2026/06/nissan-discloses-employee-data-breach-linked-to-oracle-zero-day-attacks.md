## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted nissan discloses employee data breach linked to oracle zero-day attacks. Nissan is warning that it suffered a data breach affecting current and former employees after threat actors exploited an Oracle PeopleSoft vulnerability in data theft attacks previously linked to the ShinyHunters extortion group. In breach notifications filed with the California Attorney General's Office, Oracle says these data theft attacks impacted hundreds of companies and that Nissan was specifically targeted in the campaign.
