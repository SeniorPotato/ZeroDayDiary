## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted lastpass confirms data breach in klue supply chain attack. LastPass announced that hackers accessed customer data from its Salesforce environment after stealing the company's OAuth tokens in the Klue supply chain attack earlier this month. The password management platform says its products, services, and infrastructure were not affected by the incident and that customer vaults remained secure.
