## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted blackfield ransomware asks nidec corporation for $2 million ransom. Nidec is a leader in producing motors of all sizes, from micro-precision ones used in phones and hard drives to heavy-duty motors for robotics, elevators, and large HVAC systems.
