## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted maine disables data breach notification portal after fake disclosures. Maine has taken its public data breach reporting portal offline after fraudulent breach disclosures were published on the state's website, prompting a review of procedures to prevent abuse in the future. Yesterday, BleepingComputer reported that fake data breach disclosures had been submitted to Maine's official breach notification portal impersonating Discord and the multiplayer social virtual reality platform VRChat.
