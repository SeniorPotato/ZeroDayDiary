## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted who runs the ransomware group ‘the gentlemen?’. A cybercrime group known as The Gentlemen has emerged as the second most active ransomware gang by victim count, rapidly attracting a talented pool of hackers through an aggressive recruitment strategy that promises affiliates 90 percent of any ransom paid by victims. A graphic created and shared by The Gentlemen ransomware group administrator Hastalamuerte on Breachforums in May 2026.
