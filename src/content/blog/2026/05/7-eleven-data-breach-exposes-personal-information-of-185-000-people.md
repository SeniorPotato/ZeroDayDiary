## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted 7-eleven data breach exposes personal information of 185,000 people. The ShinyHunters extortion gang stole the personal information of over 183,000 people after hacking the systems of convenience store chain giant 7-Eleven in April, according to data breach notification service Have I Been Pwned. Founded in 1927, 7-Eleven now operates, franchises, and licenses more than 86,000 stores worldwide, including 13,000 stores in the U.S.
