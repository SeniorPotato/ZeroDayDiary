## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted nvidia confirms geforce now data breach affecting armenian users. NVIDIA has confirmed in a statement for BleepingComputer that GeForce NOW user information has been exposed in a data breach. The gaming and hardware giant has clarified that the impact is limited to Armenia, and was caused by a compromise of the infrastructure operated by a regional partner.
