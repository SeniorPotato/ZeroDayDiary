## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted hackers earn $1,298,250 for 47 zero-days at pwn2own berlin 2026. The Pwn2Own Berlin 2026 hacking contest has concluded, with security researchers collecting $1,298,250 in rewards after exploiting 47 zero-day flaws. The competition took place at the OffensiveCon conference from May 14 to May 16 and focused on enterprise technologies and artificial intelligence.

## Why it matters
This matters because AI-related risk increasingly shows up through deployment choices, interfaces, and governance gaps rather than model headlines alone.
