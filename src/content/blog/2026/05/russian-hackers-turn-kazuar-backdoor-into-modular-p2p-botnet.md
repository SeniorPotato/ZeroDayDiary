## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted russian hackers turn kazuar backdoor into modular p2p botnet. The Russian hacker group Secret Blizzard has developed its long-running Kazuar backdoor into a modular peer-to-peer (P2P) botnet designed for long-term persistence, stealth, and data collection. Secret Blizzard, whose activity overlaps that of Turla, Uroburos, and Venomous Bear, has been associated with the Russian intelligence service (FSB) and is known for targeting government and diplomatic organizations, defense-related entities, and critical systems across Europe, Asia, and Ukraine.
