## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted daemon tools trojanized in supply-chain attack to deploy backdoor. Hackers trojanized installers for the DAEMON Tools software and since April 8, delivered a backdoor to thousands of systems that downloaded the product from the official website. The supply-chain attack led to thousands of infections in more than 100 countries.
