## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted bigcommerce alerts merchants of data breach linked to ribon apps. Ecommerce platform BigCommerce has alerted multiple merchants to data breaches after attackers compromised credentials for third-party Ribon applications and used them to inject malicious scripts into online stores. The cloud-based Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) ecommerce platform confirmed the credential compromise on September 17 and immediately removed the apps to protect its customers.
