## What happened
The latest analysis post sets out a development that is directly relevant to security operators. Modern messaging apps allow users to link their phone accounts to their computer desktop. Apps such as WhatsApp Web and Signal Desktop allow people to use their accounts on other devices, such as laptops or desktop computers.

This matters because it has practical implications for defensive prioritisation, exposure management, or incident response rather than sitting as abstract security commentary.
