## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted mathspace discloses data breach affecting over 1 million people. Online maths learning platform Mathspace disclosed over the weekend that attackers stole data from more than 1 million students, staff, and parents after breaching its Metabase internal reporting system. Founded in Sydney in 2010, Mathspace is now used by thousands of schools across Australia, New Zealand, the United States, and the United Kingdom (3,432 in Australia and 3,557 abroad according to statistics reported by the company in 2023).
