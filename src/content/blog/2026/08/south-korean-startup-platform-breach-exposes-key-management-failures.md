## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted south korean startup platform breach exposes key management failures. In July, South Korea’s government-backed startup support platform, Modu-ui Changup (모두의창업), suffered a data breach. The platform supports a nationwide startup audition program overseen by South Korea’s Ministry of SMEs and Startups (MSS), and it stores participants’ personal information, including startup ideas, email addresses, and names.
