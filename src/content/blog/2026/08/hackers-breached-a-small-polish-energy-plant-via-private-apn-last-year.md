## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted hackers breached a small polish energy plant via private apn last year. Hackers used a dedicated mobile gateway to compromise a second facility during the destructive cyberattacks that hit Poland's energy sector last year. The second target was a small combined heat-and-power (CHP) plant that supplies heat to around 50,000 residents, resulting in the steam turbine and the water treatment system being shut down.
