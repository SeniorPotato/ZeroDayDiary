## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted new evooo1bot linux botnet turns routers into traffic relay nodes. The malware's capabilities extend beyond turning devices into proxy nodes and include credential theft, SSH brute-forcing, and launching distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks.
