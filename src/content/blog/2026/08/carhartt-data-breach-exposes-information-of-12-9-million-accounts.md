Recent reporting highlighted carhartt data breach exposes information of 12.9 million accounts. Founded in 1889, Carhartt is an American apparel company with workwear and streetwear manufacturing facilities in Kentucky and Tennessee and more than 3,000 employees in the United States and Europe.
