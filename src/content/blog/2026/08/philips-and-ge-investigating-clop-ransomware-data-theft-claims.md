## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted philips and ge investigating clop ransomware data theft claims. Tech giants General Electric (GE) and Philips have also confirmed they're investigating claims that the Clop ransomware gang breached their systems and stole data. While a GE spokesperson said the company is aware of the claim and is "working to assess the potential issue," a Philips spokesperson confirmed its systems were breached but said the incident has been contained and didn't affect customers.
