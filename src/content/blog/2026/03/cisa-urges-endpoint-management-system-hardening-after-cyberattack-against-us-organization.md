## What happened
The latest cisa and partner-agency guidance sets out a development that is directly relevant to security operators. CISA is aware of malicious cyber activity targeting endpoint management systems of U.S. To defend against similar malicious activity that misuses legitimate endpoint management software, CISA urges organizations to implement Microsoft’s newly released best practices for securing Microsoft Intune; the principles of these recommendations can be applied to Intune and more broadly to other endpoint management software:.

This matters because it changes what privacy teams, platform owners, or product leaders should treat as a real operating constraint. It is a direct signal about how compliance and policy expectations are being translated into implementation work.
