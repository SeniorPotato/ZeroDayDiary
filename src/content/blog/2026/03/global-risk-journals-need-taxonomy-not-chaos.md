A global event journal becomes more valuable over time only if its structure is stable enough to support comparison. Without a clear taxonomy, archives become noise: events are hard to cluster, patterns disappear, and important developments are trapped inside one-off headlines.

Taxonomy is not administrative overhead. It is part of the editorial product. Categories and tags make it possible to see where surveillance is expanding, where breach patterns repeat, where regulatory pressure is intensifying, and where AI governance keeps failing in similar ways.

That is why ZeroDayDiary should behave more like a monitored ledger than a generic blog. Clear naming, consistent categories, and disciplined tagging are part of the intelligence function.
