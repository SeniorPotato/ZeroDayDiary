## Assessment
The strongest signal here is that regulator expectations are being expressed through enforceable outcomes rather than soft signalling alone. In practice, that means cloud-adjacent control planes, shared services, and inherited trust assumptions deserve more scrutiny than many organisations currently give them.

## Recommended actions
- check whether cloud services, connectors, or shared administrative paths create avoidable trust-boundary risk
- translate the development into specific ownership, policy, and evidence requirements instead of leaving it as background policy tracking
- test whether current controls, age checks, notices, or governance workflows would withstand regulator scrutiny in a similar case
- monitor follow-on reporting or primary-source updates for scope expansion, implementation guidance, or stronger enforcement signals
