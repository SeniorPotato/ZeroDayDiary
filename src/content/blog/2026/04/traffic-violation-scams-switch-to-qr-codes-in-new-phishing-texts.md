## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted traffic violation scams switch to qr codes in new phishing texts. Scammers are sending fake "Notice of Default" traffic violation text messages impersonating state courts across the U.S., pressuring recipients to scan a QR code that leads to a phishing site demanding a $6.99 payment while stealing personal and financial information. This is a new variation of the widely sent toll violation and unpaid parking ticket scams that users received in 2025, which claimed to be from state toll agencies.
