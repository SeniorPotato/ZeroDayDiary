## What happened
Recent reporting highlighted apple fixes ios bug that retained deleted notification data. Apple has released out-of-band security updates for iPhone and iPad devices to fix a Notification Services flaw that could allow notifications marked for deletion to remain stored on the device. The bug, tracked as CVE-2026-28950, was fixed on April 22, 2026, in iOS 26.4.2 and iPadOS 26.4.2 and in iOS 18.7.8 and iPadOS 18.7.8.
