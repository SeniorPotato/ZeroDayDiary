# Curated Sources

## Tier 1
- CISA Cybersecurity Advisories
- CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog
- NCSC (UK) news
- ICO News and Blogs
- EDPB News
- ENISA News
- FTC Press Releases
- ISO Insights and Updates
- Europol
- official company incident disclosures
- court and regulator notices

## Automated Tier 1 source spine
The structured automated review pipeline currently monitors:
- CISA Cybersecurity Advisories
- CISA KEV Catalog
- ICO News and Blogs
- EDPB News
- ENISA News
- NCSC UK News
- FTC Press Releases
- ISO Insights and Updates

## Tier 2
- trusted cyber trade press
- privacy investigative outlets
- policy / governance reporting
- major news wires

## Tier 3
- aggregators
- general tech reporting without primary sourcing
- social posts used only as discovery prompts

## Notes
- ICO, EDPB, and ISO are explicitly included in the automated review cycle.
- Prefer sources that expose stable article links, advisories, or official notices.
- Avoid adding blocked or highly dynamic sources until the parser is adapted for them.
